Pines, Popovers & Park Loops
3 Days in Acadia
12 juillet 20267 min read
Some trips are about the food. This one was about the air. The kind of crisp, piney, salty-off-the-ocean air that makes you take a stupidly deep breath and think “Wow, I should really go outside more.”
We spent three days hiking, camping, and generally pretending to be the outdoorsy people we wish we were full-time (thanks 9-5 jobs!). Acadia delivered in every way — golden light on granite, and the kind of quiet that makes your phone feel irrelevant. (I still took 400 photos. Obviously.)
Stop one
Jordan Pond
popovers, and the world’s most photogenic lawn
swipe — 6 photos
The popovers are the point. I know, I know — you drove into a national park for hot buttered air. But these are the real pépites: golden, hollow, faintly eggy, served with strawberry jam on a lawn that looks photoshopped.
If you only do one thing
Park Loop Road at sunrise, popovers straight after. Non-negotiable.
Also there was lobster. There’s always lobster. (And let’s be honest, I’d have gone back for a third roll if the queue hadn’t judged me.)
xx lele
